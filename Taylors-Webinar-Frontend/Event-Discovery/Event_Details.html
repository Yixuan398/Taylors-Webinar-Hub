About this event

Open to all Taylor’s students

What does the next wave of artificial intelligence mean for the way we learn, work and create? Join Taylor's Computing Society for an afternoon exploring AI in 2026, from the rise of autonomous agents and multimodal models to the real-world decisions shaping responsible AI.

Through practical demos, a hands-on workshop and an open Q&A, you’ll discover how to turn emerging tools into useful projects. Bring your laptop, your questions and a curious mindset. Whether you’re writing your first line of code or building your next prototype, there’s a place for you in the conversation.
